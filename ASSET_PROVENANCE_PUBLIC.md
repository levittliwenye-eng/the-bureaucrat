# Public Asset Provenance

The public source repository contains the ten runtime PNG files under
`UI/v3/runtime/` and one plug-in UI capture under `Docs/Images/`. They were
prepared specifically for this plug-in from the project's generated interface
artwork. The UI capture is rendered directly by the project's own test target.
No stock-image file, commercial font file, third-party logo file, audio sample,
impulse response, or model weight is included in the public asset set.

Development references, user-supplied screenshots, QA captures, design targets,
marketing videos, and demonstration audio are excluded from the public source
repository. Their exclusion avoids implying redistribution rights that have not
been granted for the public release.

The runtime artwork is distributed under the terms described in
`ASSETS_LICENSE.md`. Historical-looking visual elements form part of a fictional
industrial interface and do not indicate institutional endorsement.

# Privacy

THE BUREAUCRAT processes audio locally inside the host application.

The plug-in:

- requires no account or activation;
- makes no network requests;
- contains no telemetry, analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking;
- does not collect audio, MIDI, projects, file paths, device identifiers, or
  personal information;
- stores only parameter and preset state through the host's project system.

Disconnec audio and Pravda Audio do not receive plug-in state or usage data.
Git hosting, downloads, issue reports, and voluntary support messages are
handled by the services through which a user chooses to provide them and are
subject to those services' privacy terms.

Effective date: 2026-08-18.
